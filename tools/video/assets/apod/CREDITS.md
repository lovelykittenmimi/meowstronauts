# APOD stills for the 30-second video

Source: NASA APOD on science.nasa.gov, downloaded 2026-11-14.
These images are **video-only**; they are not game assets and are not in the game build.

## Chosen

### Mars globe — Sol 2, before the crash

- File: `mars-globe-viking-2024-11-10.jpg` (1552 × 1552)
- Title: *Valles Marineris: The Grand Canyon of Mars* (APOD 2024-11-10)
- Credit: **NASA / USGS / Viking Project**
- Page: https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2024-november-10-valles-marineris-the-grand-canyon-of-mars/
- Use: full-disk Mars on black; centre it on 1920×1080 and let the black background bleed into the frame. Do not stretch to 16:9.

### Solar flare — Sol 2, the hit

- File: `sun-flare-sdo-2017-09-14.jpg` (4096 × 4096)
- Title: *Flare Well AR2673* (APOD 2017-09-14)
- Credit: **NASA / SDO / AIA, EVE and HMI science teams**
- Page: https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2017-september-14-flare-well-ar2673/
- Use: scale to 1080×1080, pad on black to 1920×1080. Do not crop to 16:9 — the flare sits on the right limb and would be cut.
- **Do not label this as the 14 May 2025 X2.7 flare.** It is illustrative B-roll; the X2.7 claim belongs only to the DONKI data panel.

### Coronal mass ejection — swap for the flare if a wider storm reads better

- File: `sun-cme-soho-2016-01-10.jpg` (1875 × 2100)
- Title: *Sun Storm: A Coronal Mass Ejection* (APOD 2016-01-10)
- Credit: **NASA / ESA / SOHO Consortium**
- Page: https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2016-january-10-sun-storm-a-coronal-mass-ejection/
- Use: crop a 16:9 band through the sun; the horizontal rays survive. Softer than the SDO still when enlarged.
